# Goal and Scope

## Goal

Song Recall helps one person test memory of songs already available in that person's iPhone Music library. It starts local playback, measures time to answer, and gives faster correct recall a higher score.

## MVP scope

- iPhone-only experience.
- iOS 26+.
- Apple Music library authorization.
- Locally playable library tracks.
- Ten random rounds.
- Thirty-second round timer.
- Free-text answer.
- Weighted score.
- Results and replay.
- Local-only operation.

## Non-goals

- Spotify or another streaming service.
- Music recognition.
- Lyrics lookup.
- Internet metadata.
- Accounts, sync, cloud storage, analytics, ads, or leaderboards.
- Files-app importing in first MVP.
- Persistent history in first MVP.

## Product quality bar

Small feature set, fast launch, clear feedback, strong accessibility, and polished native iOS interaction.
